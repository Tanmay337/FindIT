Why keep titles vague?

During campus research, finders reported that naming exact brands or posting public photos attracted false claims and troll messages.

  • Good title: “Black Charger” or “Earbuds Case”
  • Avoid: Brand, model number, or stickers in public fields
  • Good secret question: “What is written on the tape?” or “What colour keychain is attached?”

Custody Badge Guide

  • Held by Finder Item stays with you until ownership is approved.
  • At Guard Booth Deposited at a security cabin or building desk.
  • Left on Bench Sighting logged without picking up the item.

Demonstration Mode & Data Persistence

This interactive prototype runs entirely in your browser using localStorage. Reports, claims, handover updates, and notifications persist across page refreshes on this device.

Demo vs. Production: This static build does not provide real campus SSO authentication, server-side encryption of private verification answers, or cross-device synchronization. In a production campus deployment, verification answers, uploaded photos, and handover messages would be stored and access-controlled on a secure backend server.
